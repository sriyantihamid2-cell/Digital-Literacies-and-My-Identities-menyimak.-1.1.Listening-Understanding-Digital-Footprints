Digital Literacies and My Identities: Understanding Digital Footprints

Part 1: Understanding Digital Footprints

Welcome, students of Madrasah Aliyah! Today we will learn how to build our digital identity. Read the transcript below carefully. You can practice this dialogue with your group members!

📖 Dialogue Transcript: A Visit to Danau Limboto

Rina: "The sunset at Danau Limboto is breathtaking today! I should live-stream it on my Instagram and share my exact location so everyone can join us."

Budi: "Wait, Rina. Remember what we learned in Mr. Andi's class about Digital Footprints? Sharing your live location might not be safe."

Rina: "Digital footprint? You mean like my internet search history?"

Budi: "Yes, but there are two types. An Active Digital Footprint is what you purposely share, like your photos, comments, or a live-stream. A Passive Digital Footprint is data collected without you knowing, like a website tracking your IP address."

Rina: "Oh, I see. So, everything I post online forms my digital identity?"

Budi: "Exactly! And once it's on the internet, it's very hard to erase. It could affect our future, like when applying for universities. A positive footprint, like sharing a video about Gorontalo culture, is great. But oversharing private info or bad behavior leaves a negative footprint."

Rina: "Wow, I should be more careful. I will just post a beautiful picture later with a good caption about nature conservation."

🔑 Key Vocabulary & Phrases:

  • Digital Footprint: Jejak digital (rekaman aktivitas kita di internet).
  • Active Footprint: Jejak yang sengaja kita tinggalkan (posting, komentar, upload).
  • Passive Footprint: Jejak yang tertinggal tanpa disadari (cookies, IP tracker).
  • Digital Identity: Identitas digital (reputasi online kita).
  • Oversharing: Membagikan terlalu banyak informasi pribadi di internet.
